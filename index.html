TwelveWeek — Free 12-Week Workout Plans

TwelveWeek is a free, offline-first workout tracker built around 12-week programs. Pick a plan by level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced), focus (Full Body, Abs, Core, Legs, Strength, Upper Body), and equipment (home or gym), then follow guided sessions with a timer, voice cues, and real exercise demo videos and images. No account, no ads, no sign-up.

Why TwelveWeek

  • 36+ structured 12-week programs, filterable by level, focus, equipment, and time per day
  • Guided sessions: timed and rep-based movements, rest periods, voice cues
  • Real exercise demonstrations (video and images) via ExerciseDB and wger
  • Offline-first — programs, timers, and progress work without a connection
  • Installable as a full-screen app (PWA), no app store required
  • Free, no account, no ads, no tracking
  • Also available as a free Android app

Frequently asked questions

Is TwelveWeek free to use?

Yes. TwelveWeek is completely free, with no ads and no account required.

Does TwelveWeek work offline?

Yes. Programs, timers, and progress tracking all work without an internet connection. A connection is only needed to load exercise demo videos and images.

Do I need to install anything?

No. TwelveWeek runs in any modern browser. You can also add it to your home screen for a full-screen, app-like experience.

Is there an Android app?

Yes. TwelveWeek is also available as a free Android app with the same programs and progress tracking.
